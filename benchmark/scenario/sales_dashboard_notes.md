# Dashboard desk note — RW, January 2025

Sales is measured on what we delivered. Revenue uses delivery date, after
line discounts, without VAT or freight. Cancelled orders do not enter.

After the closed-month disputes, deliveries now stay gross of returns.
Returns are beside them by warehouse receipt month for commercial follow-up;
later returns do not reduce an earlier month's delivered result.

Margin uses standard cost per the pricing team's file, effective at delivery.
Pricing revises standards in January and July. The nightly load finishes at
02:10 WIB for the previous day's deliveries. August is complete after the
1 September load; nothing is back-dated into the delivery population.
