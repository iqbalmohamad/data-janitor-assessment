# Monthly close — DA

September 2026 checklist for August:

- Finish warehouse return confirmations through 31 August.
- Post outstanding credits in the 2–4 September batches. Receipt by month
  end and posting by close means the credit belongs to the receipt period.
- Revenue follows invoice posting period. No accrual for unbilled deliveries;
  do not back-date sales invoices. Line discounts are already netted.
- Merchandise only: VAT out, freight separate. Credits exclude freight.
- Cost follows the actual unit cost captured on the original invoice line.
  Credit quantities reverse that same original cost.
- Reconcile document totals and sign the extract: 7 September, 17:30 WIB.

Usual monthly routine: credit batches on business days 2–4; close on the
fifth business day. Keep the closed extract with its component columns.
