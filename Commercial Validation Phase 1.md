1. Product Owner reviews and merges the package PR, confirms the proposed price
   and delivery window, sets outreach dates, and supplies real warm contacts.
2. Prioritize recent reporting disputes with an executive decision and reachable
   evidence owner. Use the qualification definition; log exclusions and refusals.
3. Conduct discovery, then show the existing sample and offer. Record observations,
   objections, unprompted price evidence and response to the proposed price separately.
4. Review after each batch of five counted conversations. Preserve contrary
   evidence; version any offer change with its reason. Do not reset the denominator.
5. At 15, write a brief PO decision from the tracker: continue commercial testing,
   reshape the offer, or park it. Cite record IDs and distinguish distinct companies
   from contacts and follow-ups. An earlier decisive negative pattern may justify
   stopping; record the smaller sample rather than claiming 15.

## Commercial gate and evidence limits

Fifteen conversations complete this learning assignment, not the commercial
success gate. Apply Amendment §AB: evidence such as one paid engagement around
or above Rp30M with deposit or PO together with a second commercial commitment
from an unrelated organization; or equivalently strong partner-led evidence. In
either case, the gate also requires
evidence that diagnosis itself has value. Interest and hypothetical price acceptance
are not purchases. Gate changes remain Product Owner decisions.

Assess pain, decision urgency, workaround adequacy, buyer/budget, willingness to
pay for diagnosis alone, access friction and repeatability. Track delivery-hour
estimates as estimates; actual delivery hours remain unknown until a separately
authorized real engagement. No paid pilot is executed by this assignment.

Park or reshape if qualified buyers consistently expect free diagnosis, only fund
implementation, solve disputes adequately internally, lack urgency, or cannot
provide evidence economically (§AC). Explain dissent and missing evidence before
recommending continuation. A passed commercial gate does not itself start M1;
further engineering needs explicit Product Owner authorization.
