# What counts as a qualified buyer conversation

This operational definition implements SPEC Amendment 001 §§E–F and AA. The
six-month recency window and counting rules below are Phase 1 research choices,
not changes to the product specification. Record exceptions explicitly.

Count a completed conversation only when all five conditions are documented:

1. **Relevant organization:** an Indonesian mid-market transactional business with
   meaningful data and at least two reporting surfaces. Roughly 100–1,500 employees
   is guidance, not a hard rule. Record size/industry uncertainty or an ICP exception.
2. **Firsthand participant:** a CFO, COO, founder, finance/reporting leader, Head of
   Data or equivalent who owns the decision or directly investigated the dispute.
   Record whether they control budget, influence it, or cannot identify the buyer.
3. **Real incident:** a named management metric differed across named reports or
   teams within the past six months, or remains unresolved from earlier. Record
   incident date/period, business decision, and why it mattered to the participant.
4. **Substantive evidence:** the conversation covers the workaround and outcome,
   urgency, sponsor/budget route, diagnosis-only value, price response, objections
   and next step. “Unknown,” “no budget” and refusal to answer are valid recorded
   findings; unanswered fields without explanation are incomplete.
5. **Auditable record:** date, company alias, participant role, firsthand incident
   note, evidence/quote references, and qualification rationale are captured.

Count at most one completed qualifying conversation per organization toward 15.
Additional stakeholders and follow-ups strengthen that organization's evidence;
track them separately. An introduction, unanswered outreach, demo-only chat,
generic positive opinion or hearsay referral does not count. Screened-out contacts
remain in the index with a reason.

**Negative evidence counts.** An internally resolved incident, no urgency, no budget,
refusal to buy diagnosis, or inaccessible evidence must not disqualify an otherwise
qualified interview. Unresolved pain with a live decision and sponsor is preferred
for prospect prioritization, not required for a favorable-looking interview sample.

A qualified conversation is not a qualified sales opportunity or proof of demand.
Mark an opportunity separately only when the buyer identifies a consequential
need, plausible sponsor/access route and concrete agreed commercial next step.
Never require willingness to pay to count an interview.

Use **qualified / incomplete / excluded / follow-up** in the tracker, with the
reason and PO review date. Report counted organizations, total conversations,
exclusions and unknowns separately. The warm-network sample is directional evidence,
not a statistically representative estimate of Indonesian buyer demand.
