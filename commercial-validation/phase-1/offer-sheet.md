# Reporting Reliability Audit

**A proposed fixed-scope engagement for management numbers that disagree.**

When Finance, Sales or a management report shows a different number, establish
why it differs and which definition fits the decision you need to make.

## When this is useful

For an Indonesian mid-market CFO, COO, founder or reporting sponsor facing a
recent, material disagreement that affects a board pack, forecast, pricing or
another management decision. A reporting owner and evidence contact participate.

## Proposed pilot scope

- One consequential reporting workflow and an agreed reporting period.
- Up to **3 named management metrics**, **10 critical data objects/tables**,
  **2 reporting surfaces**, and **3 stakeholder interviews**.
- One written reconciliation and one final readout.

## What you receive

For each metric: a **RECONCILED, CONFLICTING or UNVERIFIABLE** verdict; evidence
coverage and limitations; observed definitions; quantified reporting differences;
and a recommended basis for the stated decision. Differences are explained through
definition, timing, cost basis or errors where the evidence supports them.

The report separates measured discrepancies from inferred business consequences
and sets out correction priorities, accountable owners to agree, and a re-check
path. Missing evidence stays explicit. A single universal definition or a fully
reconciled result is not guaranteed.

## Timing, fee and access

**Proposed validation anchor: Rp30 million for the bounded pilot.** This is a
price to discuss, not observed buyer willingness to pay or an accepted quotation.
Final scope, fee, payment and tax terms require a written proposal.

**Proposed delivery target: 10 business days** after scope agreement and receipt
of usable evidence and stakeholder availability. This target has not been proven
on a client engagement. Access gaps require an explicit revised plan.

Agree read-only access or a client-run evidence option, minimum necessary data,
and handling arrangements before access. Remediation execution, ongoing monitoring,
new software and integrations are outside this engagement.

## See the method; discuss your case

The [six-page Northstar sample audit](../../report/samples/northstar-august-2026-audit.pdf)
is a **synthetic demonstration, not a client case study**. It covers three fictional
surfaces; this proposed pilot is limited to two. Demo discrepancies are not customer
losses, savings, or evidence of commercial demand.
