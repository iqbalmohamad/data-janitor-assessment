acceptance is not a purchase. Note whether committing organizations are unrelated.

## Batch and final readout — complete after evidence exists

Record IDs included; counted organizations / total contacts; repeated pain and
adequate workarounds; diagnosis-only value; price responses by offer version;
objections; access friction; commitments and their evidence; contrary findings;
missing evidence; and PO decision with next owner/date. Keep estimates of future
delivery hours separate from actual hours, which remain untested until a separately
authorized engagement. No automatic score or proceed threshold is calculated.
