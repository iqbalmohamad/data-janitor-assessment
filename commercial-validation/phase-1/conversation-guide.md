# Buyer conversation guide

Use with the [qualification criteria](qualification.md), [offer](offer-sheet.md)
and [tracker](conversation-tracker.md). Aim for 30–40 minutes. Ask about the real
incident before showing Northstar or a price. Permission to take private notes is
not permission to publish quotes, record the call or name the company.

## 1. Firsthand incident and consequence — 10 minutes

Open: “Saya sedang menguji apakah rekonsiliasi angka secara independen cukup
bernilai untuk dibayar. Saya ingin memahami kejadian nyata; jawaban bahwa tim
internal sudah menyelesaikannya juga sangat membantu.”

- When did management numbers last disagree? Which metric, reporting period,
  reports or teams, and what was your own involvement?
- What decision was affected? Who noticed, what happened next, and what deadline
  or meeting mattered? What happens if it remains unresolved?
- What was the size of the difference, if known? Is that measured, estimated or
  unknown? What evidence supports the claimed consequence?
- Was this a one-off or recurring issue? If resolved, how and how satisfactorily?

Do not equate a reporting gap with lost cash. Capture the respondent's account
separately from independently verified facts; no confidential documents are needed.

## 2. Workaround, urgency, buying and access — 10 minutes

- Walk me through the last attempt to resolve it. Who did the work, for how long,
  using which reports? What worked and what remains unsolved?
- What did you actually spend internally or externally? Separate remembered cost,
  estimates and documented spend. Why would you change that approach now?
- Who owns the decision, who would sponsor external diagnosis, and who approves
  spending? Are you that person? Which budget/process would apply?
- Is anything already funded or being procured? What approval steps and dates
  would matter? An unknown budget is a finding, not a reason to invent a range.
- Who could provide report logic, underlying evidence and definitions? Could a
  bounded read-only or client-run review be permitted? What access/procurement
  friction would prevent it? Do not request credentials or data on this call.

## 3. Independent diagnosis and the offer — 10 minutes

Before sharing price: “What output would change your decision? Would you fund
diagnosis and a written reconciliation if your team handled the fixes? Why or
why not? What have you paid for comparable help, and what amount could be justified?”

Then introduce Northstar: “This is fictional data, not a customer success story.”
Use page 1 for the decision, pages 2–3 for explaining differences, and page 4 for
evidence and ownership. Pages 5–6 support technical questions. Finance and Sales
can each be legitimate in their own contexts; do not promise to find a broken query.

Present [offer v1](offer-sheet.md), including the two-surface cap, proposed Rp30M
fee and unproven 10-business-day target. State that the sample's three surfaces
do not expand the pilot scope.

- For your incident, would you reject, consider, or pursue this scope at Rp30M?
  What drives that answer? What would need to change?
- Does the diagnosis have value on its own, or only when bundled with fixes?
- What is the strongest reason to decline: adequate workaround, urgency, trust,
  access, scope, price, budget authority, or something else? Ask openly first;
  offer examples only if needed.
- If you want to proceed, who else must join and what concrete step can you take?

Record unprompted amounts separately from reactions after the price anchor. “Sounds
good” is interest, not willingness-to-pay proof. A discount or different scope is
a new offer version, not acceptance of this one. Do not negotiate delivery commitments
or promise features to rescue an objection.

## 4. Close and record — 5 minutes

Reflect back the incident and main constraint; invite correction. Agree one next
action with an owner and date: sponsor introduction, scoped proposal discussion,
evidence-access discussion, later follow-up, or explicit no-fit/no action.
Ask for an introduction only if appropriate and welcome.

Immediately complete the evidence card, qualification decision and exclusion reason
if any. Save brief permission-appropriate quotes, unknowns and contrary evidence.
Distinguish verbal interest, a proposal request, written commercial commitment and
deposit/PO evidence. Follow-ups enrich the same record and do not increase the count.

After every five counted conversations, compare why buyers would pay for diagnosis
with why they would not. Report actual denominators; do not treat warm-network
responses as representative market demand.
